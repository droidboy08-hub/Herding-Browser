# Support

## Getting around

Herding has almost no chrome on purpose. Everything is a gesture or the one
button.

- **Open Home** — drag down near the top of a page. Home is the box with the
  address field, your tabs, history, downloads and settings.
- **Back and forward** — swipe in from the left or right edge, as in Safari.
- **Reload** — tap the round button in the bottom corner.
- **Page menu** — press and hold that same button. Share, bookmark, print,
  desktop site, and the rest.

The downward drag deliberately ignores video players, so scrubbing or dismissing
a video never opens Home by accident. If it still feels too eager or too stubborn,
**Settings → Home → Swipe sensitivity** adjusts how far you have to drag.

## Blocking

**Settings → Privacy → Content Blocking** chooses how much is blocked. Higher
levels block more and break more sites. If a page misbehaves, try a lower level
before assuming the site is broken.
