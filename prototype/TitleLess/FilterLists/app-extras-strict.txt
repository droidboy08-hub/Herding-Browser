! Title: App Extras (Aggressive)
! Description: Rules written for this browser that only apply at the Aggressive
!   level. Everything here blocks something the community lists deliberately
!   leave alone, so it stays out of the levels people run by default.
!
! The bar for this file is lower than for app-extras.txt — these need only be
! ad or tracking infrastructure — but it is not zero. Nothing goes in that
! serves page content.

! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! DoubleClick subdomains the community lists miss.
!
! EasyList blocks `doubleclick.net` host by host rather than as a whole domain,
! and deliberately: the same domain carries server-side ad insertion for video,
! which the lists exempt by name —
!
!   @@||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/ssai/
!   @@||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/ondemand/hls/
!
! A blanket `||doubleclick.net^` would take those with it and stop video
! playing on every site using DAI. Naming the hosts individually is what makes
! these safe.
!
! `stats.g`, `ad.` and `static.` are already covered upstream. These two are
! not: the mobile ad host, and the MediaVisor campaign-management platform.
||m.doubleclick.net^
||mediavisor.doubleclick.net^

! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Analytics hosts with no upstream rule at all.
!
! `click.googleanalytics.com` appears in none of the bundled lists, under any
! modifier. It is the outbound-link click tracker — its only job is recording
! that you left.
!
! Note what is *not* here: a broad `||google-analytics.com^`. Upstream scopes
! that host to `$script,third-party,xmlhttprequest` and then carries a long list
! of `@@` exceptions for sites that break without analytics.js. Those exceptions
! live in EasyPrivacy, and a `WKContentRuleList` exception cannot reach across
! into another compiled list — so a blanket rule here would be unexemptable and
! would break every site on that list.
||click.googleanalytics.com^

! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Meta's Audience Network, which no bundled list names.
!
! `an.facebook.com` serves Meta's ads *into other people's* sites and apps. The
! sibling hosts are already covered — `||analytics.facebook.com^$third-party`,
! `||pixel.facebook.com^` — and this one was simply missed.
!
! `$third-party` for the same reason upstream uses it on the siblings: the rule
! then cannot fire while you are actually on facebook.com, where this host is
! first-party and not ours to interfere with.
||an.facebook.com^$third-party
